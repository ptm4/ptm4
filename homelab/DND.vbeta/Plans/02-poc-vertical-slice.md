# 02: POC vertical slice

**Status 2026-09-09: built and running.** `Assets/Dungine/POC/POC.unity` is generated by
`Dungine.EditorTools.PocSceneBuilder` (menu **Dungine > POC > Build Scene**; rebuild it, never
hand-edit the scene). Scripts in `Assets/Dungine/POC/`: `GridMap`, `Unit`, `Billboard`,
`CameraRig`, `TorchFlicker`, `TurnManager` (initiative, move, melee, goblin AI, hotseat,
seeded RNG), `PocHud` (IMGUI), `SpriteAnimator` (plays the bible sheet: idle/walk/attack/
hit/death by material offset, facing from movement direction, W = E mirrored; verified
cycling in play mode). Placeholder sheets from `tools/make_placeholder_sprites.py`
in `Assets/Dungine/Sprites/POC/`; the goblin is the first real Codex-generated sheet
(turnaround-only placeholder, see Plan 05). Plays with zero console errors; screenshots in
`Dungine/Assets/Captures/` (gitignored). **Remaining:** Peter plays a fight to the death and
judges the look against `Plans/Digital-Influences/`.

## Learnings (feed 04 / 05 / 06 / 11)
- **Camera:** the bible's 38° pitch at 15 u puts a 10-ft wall between camera and party;
  50° / 11 u works. The real game needs **cutaway or dithering of walls that occlude the
  party** (south row is a 0.4 u ledge in the POC as a stand-in). Bible §5 updated (D28).
- **Lighting numbers** from the bible were far too dark once ACES ran: ambient
  `night_purple_3`, moon 1.6, torches intensity 40 / range 9, exposure +0.6 read correctly.
  DoF start must be beyond the camera-to-party distance (14 → 30 u at 11 u distance).
- **Sprites in a 3D URP scene:** lit billboards are URP/Lit quads with alpha clip and
  `Cull Off`; frame selection must be material tiling/offset, because the SRP Batcher ignores
  per-renderer `_BaseMap_ST` property blocks. Sprites are dark under moonlight alone; a
  character fill light or a partially-unlit sprite shader is a Plan 11/04 question.
- **Input:** Dungine is Input-System-only; legacy `Input` throws. Use `InputSystem` APIs.
- **Editor automation:** `eval` has a 5 s main-thread cap; long builders still complete
  but the reply times out, and `EditorApplication.delayCall` from eval never fires. Calling
  the static builder directly and polling the scene file works. `capture_game_view` only
  writes under `Assets/`.
- **Map authoring:** ASCII rows are north-to-south; y = height - 1 - row. Spawn cells must be
  checked against the map (first build put a goblin inside a pillar).

## Goal
Prove the *look* and the *workflow* before investing in the engine: one voxel room, four
pre-made characters with placeholder sprites, one encounter, minimal hard-coded combat
(initiative, move, attack, HP), hotseat on one machine. Everything here is disposable;
its value is what it teaches Plans 04, 05, 06, and 11.

## Scope guard
- No `engine/` code. Combat here is a throwaway MonoBehaviour; the real engine is Plan 03.
- No netcode. Relay comes in Plan 10 once the engine event log exists.
- Sprites may be hand-placeholders or the first generated batch from Plan 05.
