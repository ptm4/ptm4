# 04: Art style bible

**Status 2026-09-09: v0.1 drafted.** The bible itself is [`docs/style-bible.md`](../docs/style-bible.md);
palette and templates are generated by `tools/palette.py` and `tools/make_sprite_template.py`
(edit the scripts, never the PNGs). Decisions recorded as D27. Remaining `done_when` needs the
POC scene (Plan 02) and Peter's side-by-side approval; see the bible's §9 for review points.

## Goal
One document every generated asset must obey, so that hundreds of AI-generated sprites and
voxel tiles read as a single game. References: Octopath Traveler (HD-2D staging, tilt-shift,
bloom), Rain World (grit, organic silhouettes), Sea of Stars (sprite fidelity, dynamic
light), Cloudpunk (mood lighting, rain, voxel chunkiness) as stretch.

## Must define
- Master palette (size, ramps, how quantization maps to it).
- Sprite template: canvas size, pixels-per-unit, anchor, the 4-facing sheet layout, frame
  counts per animation (D9), outline rule, shading direction.
- Paper-doll layer order and registration.
- Voxel scale: voxels per 5-ft tile, character height in tiles, prop scale.
- Lighting/post recipe: URP volume settings for bloom, depth of field, color grading, fog,
  rain; time-of-day palette shifts.
- Portrait style (for sheets and dialogue) and UI icon style.
- Do/don't examples with images.

### Synthesis to encode in the bible
- **Two palette modes** from one master palette: *Day* (Octopath/Sea of Stars: warm, saturated,
  high-key) and *Night/Dungeon* (gothic ref/Cloudpunk/Rain World: low-value cool base, one hot
  emissive hue, one accent). Scenes declare a mode; post-processing color grading swaps.
- **Sprites**: Sea of Stars fidelity, dark outline, 3-4 shade ramp, strong silhouettes; must read
  against both palette modes (test every sprite on a day tile and a night tile).
- **World**: Cloudpunk voxels with emissive channels; terraces for elevation; wet/grainy surfaces.
- **Post recipe**: bloom (emissive-driven), tilt-shift DoF, fog volume, light shafts, grain,
  weather particles; all URP Volume profiles so the DM can pick "rainy night" per scene.
- **Focal rule**: one accent hue per scene, reserved for what the players should notice.
